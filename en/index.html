Context

What is changing

  • Art. 50 EU AI Act has applied since 2 August 2026.
  • From 2 December 2026, image generators already on the market must also embed machine-readable provenance markings (an obligation of the generator providers).
  • Deepfakes must be visibly labelled.

What the check covers

Four technical checkpoints

  • C2PA manifestIs a manifest present, and is it intact?
  • Invisible watermarkDoes the image contain a TrustMark watermark?
  • SignerWas the signature issued by an authority on the C2PA Trust List?
  • Manipulation after signingWas the image altered after it was signed?

Output: a report (HTML + PDF) with a score.
